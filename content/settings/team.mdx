# Team & Invitations

The **Team** section lets owners and admins manage who has access to your Janovix organization and what level of access they have.

{/* IMAGE: public/images/settings/settings-team.png - Team members list showing avatar, name, email, role badge, and Remove/Change Role actions */}

## Current Team Members

The team list shows all active members of your organization with:

- Their name and email
- Their current role (`owner`, `admin`, or `member`)
- The date they joined

## Roles

| Role     | What They Can Do                                                                                    |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `owner`  | Everything — including billing, ownership transfer, and organization deletion                       |
| `admin`  | All AML features, Settings management, team management; cannot change billing or transfer ownership |
| `member` | Access to AML and Watchlist apps; cannot modify Settings                                            |

## Inviting a New Team Member

{/* IMAGE: public/images/settings/settings-invite.png - Invitation dialog showing email field, role selector, and Send Invite button */}

<Steps>
### Open the Invite Dialog
Click **+ Invite Member** in the top-right of the Team page.

### Enter Email and Role

- **Email address** — The person must have a Janovix account or will be prompted to create one
- **Role** — Select `admin` or `member` (you cannot invite someone as `owner`)

### Send the Invitation

Click **Send Invitation**. The invitee receives an email with a link to accept.

### Track Pending Invitations

Pending invitations appear in a separate section below the active members list. You can cancel a pending invitation before it is accepted.

</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
	Pending invitations expire after **7 days**. If the invitee doesn't accept in
	time, resend the invitation from the Pending Invitations section.
</Callout>

## Changing a Member's Role

1. Locate the member in the team list
2. Click the role badge or the **⋯** menu
3. Select the new role
4. Confirm the change

Role changes take effect immediately.

## Removing a Member

1. Click the **⋯** menu next to the member's name
2. Select **Remove from Organization**
3. Confirm

Removed members lose access immediately. Their data (operations, clients they created, etc.) remains in the system.

## Accepting an Invitation

If you've been invited to an organization:

1. Click the link in your invitation email
2. Sign in or create a Janovix account
3. You'll be automatically redirected to the organization

You can also accept pending invitations from **Settings → Personal** under the **Pending Invitations** section. A badge in the sidebar navigation indicates pending invitations.

## Seat-Based Billing

Adding members consumes seats on your plan. The current seat count is shown at the top of the Team page. If you need more seats, upgrade your plan in [Billing & Plans](/settings/billing).
