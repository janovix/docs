# Activity Feed

The **Activity** page (`/{orgSlug}/activity`) is a dedicated notification center for your active organization. It complements in-context toasts and the notifications control in the top bar by giving you a **scrollable history** you can return to at any time.

{/* IMAGE: public/images/aml/activity-feed.png - Activity page grouped by day with severity styling and unread dots */}

## What Appears Here

Events are loaded from the **notifications service** for the organization you have selected. Typical entries include:

- Compliance and product events routed with a **title**, **body**, optional **severity**, and **callback URL**
- Items that open the relevant AML screen when you click them (for example a client, alert, or import job)

## Layout and Behavior

- **Grouped by day** — e.g. “Today”, “Yesterday”, then full dates for older days
- **Severity styling** — visual distinction for informational, warning, and error-level items
- **Unread indicator** — items you have not yet opened from the feed may show an unread marker until you interact with them
- **Click to navigate** — choosing an item follows its `callbackUrl` inside the AML app (same behavior as clicking a notification elsewhere)

## Infinite Scroll

The feed loads more items as you scroll toward the bottom (**infinite scroll** with a cursor). When there are no more events, you reach the end of the list.

## Empty and Loading States

- While the first page is loading, the page shows a loading state
- If there are **no notifications** for the period, you see an empty state instead of a blank list

<Callout type="info">
	Activity is **per organization**. Switch organizations from the header
	switcher to see a different feed. The **Dashboard** may still show high-level
	KPIs; use **Activity** when you want the chronological event stream.
</Callout>
