# Clients & KYC

The **Clients** module is the foundation of your AML compliance. Every vulnerable activity operation must be linked to a registered client with sufficient KYC data.

{/* IMAGE: public/images/aml/client-list.png - Client list view with status badges, search bar, and KYC completion filters */}

## Client Types

Janovix supports three types of clients corresponding to the LFPIORPI's definition of _Cliente o Usuaria_:

| Type                             | Description                           | RFC Length    |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------- |
| **Physical Person** (`PHYSICAL`) | Individual human clients              | 13 characters |
| **Moral Person** (`MORAL`)       | Companies, corporations, associations | 12 characters |
| **Trust** (`TRUST`)              | Fideicomiso (trust structures)        | 12 characters |

## Registering a New Client

{/* IMAGE: public/images/aml/client-form-physical.png - Client creation form for a physical person, showing required fields */}

<Steps>
### Open the Create Form
Navigate to **Clients** in the sidebar and click **+ New Client** in the top-right corner.

### Select Client Type

Choose between Physical Person, Moral Person, or Trust. The form fields will adjust based on your selection.

### Fill in Core Information

**For Physical Persons:**

- First name, paternal surname, maternal surname
- RFC (13 characters)
- Date of birth
- Nationality and country of birth
- CURP (optional)

**For Moral Persons / Trusts:**

- Business name (_razón social_)
- RFC (12 characters)
- Incorporation date
- Country of incorporation
- Tax identification in country of origin (if foreign)

### Add Contact & Address

Fill in the client's email, phone, and primary address. You can add multiple addresses after creation.

### Save and Screen

Click **Create Client**. Janovix will:

- Save the client record
- Automatically trigger a watchlist screening against OFAC, UNSC, SAT 69-B, and PEP databases
- Assign an initial KYC status based on the data provided

</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
	A client with **INCOMPLETE** KYC status cannot be linked to operations that
	require full identification under Art. 17. Complete the KYC checklist before
	recording high-value transactions.
</Callout>

## KYC Status

{/* IMAGE: public/images/aml/client-kyc-status.png - KYC status indicator showing completion percentage and missing items */}

Each client has a KYC status that reflects compliance readiness:

| Status                 | Meaning                                                  |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| `INCOMPLETE`           | Required data fields or documents are missing            |
| `PENDING_VERIFICATION` | Data is submitted but awaiting compliance officer review |
| `COMPLETE`             | All required data collected and verified                 |
| `EXPIRED`              | Previously complete but data or documents have expired   |

The KYC status considers your organization's [compliance settings](/en/settings/compliance) — specifically the **self-service required sections** — and the activity-specific thresholds from Art. 17.

## Client Detail Page

{/* IMAGE: public/images/aml/client-detail.png - Client detail page showing personal info, documents, addresses tabs and watchlist screening results */}

The client detail page is organized into tabs:

- **Overview** — Key information, KYC status, and watchlist screening results
- **Documents** — Uploaded identification documents with expiry tracking
- **Addresses** — All registered addresses (a client may have multiple)
- **Shareholders** — Ownership structure (for moral persons and trusts)
- **Beneficial Controllers** — Ultimate beneficial owners (_Beneficiarios Controladores_)
- **Operations** — All operations linked to this client
- **KYC Sessions** — History of self-service KYC sessions

## Watchlist Screening Results

Every client is automatically screened when created or when their information is updated. The screening covers:

| List         | Description                                                                     |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **PEP**      | Politically Exposed Persons database                                            |
| **OFAC SDN** | U.S. Treasury Office of Foreign Assets Control — Specially Designated Nationals |
| **UNSC**     | UN Security Council sanctions list                                              |
| **SAT 69-B** | Mexican taxpayers with non-existent operations                                  |

Screening results appear in the client's Overview tab with a match score and classification:

- `isPEP` — Client matched as a PEP
- `ofacSanctioned` — OFAC SDN match
- `unscSanctioned` — UN Security Council match
- `sat69bListed` — SAT 69-B match

<Callout type="error">
	**Never proceed with a transaction for a sanctioned client** without
	completing your internal due diligence process and, where applicable,
	notifying the relevant authorities. Janovix will not block the operation at
	the system level — compliance decisions remain with your team.
</Callout>

## Shareholders (Moral Persons / Trusts)

For moral persons and trusts, the LFPIORPI requires identifying shareholders and beneficial controllers. In the **Shareholders** tab:

- Add shareholders (individual persons or companies)
- Specify ownership percentage
- Hierarchical structure supported (sub-shareholders of a shareholder company)
- Attach identification documents

## Beneficial Controllers

In the **Beneficial Controllers** add the _Beneficiarios Controladores_ — the physical persons who ultimately control or benefit from the moral entity:

| BC Type             | Description                                     |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| `SHAREHOLDER`       | Person who owns >25% of capital                 |
| `LEGAL_REP`         | Legal representative                            |
| `TRUSTEE`           | Fiduciario (trustee in a trust)                 |
| `SETTLOR`           | Fideicomitente (settlor in a trust)             |
| `TRUST_BENEFICIARY` | Fideicomisario (beneficiary of a trust)         |
| `DIRECTOR`          | Director or board member with effective control |

Each beneficial controller is automatically screened against watchlists upon registration.

## Documents

Upload identity documents in the **Documents** tab. Supported document types vary by client type and include:

- Official ID (INE, passport)
- RFC certificate
- Proof of address
- Constitutive act (for moral persons)
- Trust deed (for trusts)
- Power of attorney

The system tracks document expiry dates and will flag a client's KYC as `EXPIRED` when documents lapse.

## Searching and Filtering Clients

In the Clients list, use the search bar and filter controls to find clients by:

- Name or business name
- RFC
- KYC status
- Watchlist screening flags (PEP, OFAC, UNSC, SAT 69-B)
- Date created

All active filters are reflected in the URL, making filtered views shareable with your team.
