# Risk Management

The **Risk** area in the AML app supports **evaluación basada en riesgo** (EBR) aligned with LFPIORPI Art. 18-VII. It combines an organization-level assessment, configurable methodology, per-client risk scores, and a history of evaluations.

{/* IMAGE: public/images/aml/risk-dashboard.png - Risk hub showing assessed clients, high-risk count, org assessment gauges, and distribution chart */}

## Where to Find It

In the sidebar, open **Compliance → Risk models** (or navigate to `/{orgSlug}/risk`).

Sub-routes:

| Path                               | Purpose                                                                |
| ---------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `/{orgSlug}/risk`                  | Risk dashboard — KPIs, org assessment summary, distribution, shortcuts |
| `/{orgSlug}/risk/assessment`       | Full organization EBR assessment and evolution                         |
| `/{orgSlug}/risk/methodology`      | View and edit effective risk methodology (scopes)                      |
| `/{orgSlug}/risk/evaluations`      | Table of client risk evaluations with filters                          |
| `/{orgSlug}/risk/evaluations/[id]` | Detail for a single evaluation                                         |

## Risk Dashboard

The hub summarizes:

- **Clients assessed** — how many clients have a risk evaluation in scope
- **High risk** — clients currently classified as high risk
- **Due for review** — assessments approaching the next review date
- **Organization assessment** — snapshot of the active EBR: inherent and residual risk, required audit type, version, and link to the full assessment page
- **Client risk distribution** — chart of counts by risk level
- **Quick actions** — jump to **Evaluations** or **Methodology**

<Callout type="info">
	Risk jobs (client batch assessment, organization assessment) are processed
	asynchronously when the assessment queue is available. The UI refreshes when
	new results are ready; you may see a short delay after triggering a run.
</Callout>

## Organization Assessment (EBR)

<Steps>
### Open the assessment page

Go to **Risk models → Organization assessment** (`/risk/assessment`).

### Review the latest EBR

The page shows the active assessment with risk elements, mitigants, and scores. You can compare **inherent** vs **residual** risk and see the **required audit type** suggested by the model.

### Evolution over time

Use the evolution view to see how assessment versions and scores changed across runs.

### Trigger a new run

When permitted, use the action to **run** or **refresh** the organization assessment. The backend queues an org assessment job; when it completes, the dashboard and this page update.

</Steps>

## Risk Methodology

The **Methodology** screen (`/risk/methodology`) shows the **effective** methodology for your organization, including its **scope**:

- **SYSTEM** — platform default
- **ACTIVITY** — tied to your organization’s vulnerable activity code
- **ORGANIZATION** — custom override saved for your org

You can **edit** categories, thresholds, and mitigants (when your role allows), **save** changes, or **reset to default** to drop the org override and fall back to activity/system defaults.

<Callout type="warning">
	Resetting methodology affects **future** evaluations only. Past evaluation
	records are kept for audit.
</Callout>

## Client Evaluations

The **Evaluations** list shows each recorded client risk assessment: client name, RFC, risk level, due diligence level, scores, trigger reason (e.g. manual vs automatic), and date.

- Filter by **risk level**, **trigger reason**, or search by client name / RFC
- Open a row to see the **evaluation detail** with parsed factor breakdowns (client, geographic, activity, transaction, mitigant factors)

From a **client** profile you can also open risk context where the UI surfaces assessments and sessions alongside KYC and screening.
