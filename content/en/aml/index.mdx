# AML Manual

The **AML** application is the core of Janovix. It is where compliance officers and their teams manage the full lifecycle of AML compliance — from onboarding clients to filing notices with the SAT.

## Navigation

The AML app is organized into two groups in the sidebar:

### Transactions

Day-to-day data management:

- **Dashboard** — KPIs, charts, and recent activity at a glance
- **Clients** — Register and manage clients (physical persons, legal entities, trusts)
- **Operations** — Record vulnerable activity transactions
- **Invoices** — Parse and manage CFDI v4.0 invoices

### Compliance

Regulatory actions:

- **Alerts** — Review automatically detected suspicious patterns
- **SAT Notices** — Generate and submit monthly notices to the SAT
- **Reports** — Create compliance and executive summary reports

### Data Management

- **Import** — Bulk-import clients and operations via CSV

<Callout type="info">
	The AML app uses **organization-based routing**. Every URL includes your
	organization slug: `https://aml.janovix.com/{org - slug}/`. If you belong to
	multiple organizations, switch between them using the organization switcher in
	the top-left corner.
</Callout>
