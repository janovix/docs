# What is Janovix?

Janovix is a cloud-native **Anti-Money Laundering (AML) compliance platform** built specifically for Mexican businesses that carry out _actividades vulnerables_ (vulnerable activities) under the [LFPIORPI](/introduction/legal-background).

The platform covers the full compliance lifecycle — from onboarding clients and recording transactions, to automatically detecting suspicious activity, generating SAT notices, and maintaining a complete audit trail.

## Platform Components

Janovix is composed of three integrated applications:

| Application   | Purpose                                                                              |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **AML**       | Manage clients, operations, alerts, notices, reports, and KYC sessions               |
| **Watchlist** | Run intelligent screening against OFAC, UNSC, SAT 69-B, PEP, and adverse media lists |
| **Settings**  | Configure your organization, team, API keys, billing, and compliance settings        |

## Who Is Janovix For?

Janovix is designed for the **obligated subjects** listed in Article 17 of the LFPIORPI — businesses operating in sectors such as:

- Real estate development and brokerage
- Vehicle sales (land, marine, and air)
- Jewelry, precious metals, and stones
- Art and antiques
- Gambling and lotteries
- Armor services
- Professional legal and notary services
- Loans and credit services
- Prepaid cards and financial instruments

## Key Capabilities

### Client Management & KYC

Register physical persons, legal entities (moral), and trusts. Track KYC completion status, collect documents, identify shareholders and beneficial controllers, and trigger automatic watchlist screening at client registration.

### Transaction Tracking (Operations)

Record all transactions covered by the LFPIORPI across 20 activity types. The system automatically calculates UMA thresholds to determine which operations require SAT notices.

### Alert Detection

Configurable rule engine that analyzes operations in real time and generates compliance alerts. Supports automatic SAT XML generation and submission tracking.

### SAT Notice Filing

Generate monthly notices in the SAT-required XML format. Track submission status and acknowledgments from the SAT portal.

### Watchlist Screening

Hybrid semantic + exact-match search across OFAC SDN, UN Security Council, SAT 69-B, PEP databases, and adverse media sources. Runs automatically on client and beneficial controller registration.

### Reporting & Analytics

Generate executive summaries, compliance status reports, transaction analyses, and alert breakdowns. Export to PDF.

### API-First

Every feature is available via a documented REST API, enabling seamless integration with your existing ERP, CRM, or notary management systems.
