# Watchlist Manual

The **Watchlist** application is available at **[watchlist.janovix.com](https://watchlist.janovix.com)**. It provides intelligent screening against multiple sanctions and compliance databases. It uses a hybrid search approach combining exact identifier matching, vector semantic search, and name similarity to deliver accurate results even with name variations, transliterations, and aliases.

## Why Watchlist Screening?

The LFPIORPI (Art. 18-I) requires obligated subjects to verify client identity, and the 2025 reforms explicitly add obligations around **Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs)**. Beyond legal requirements, screening clients against sanctions lists is essential to:

- Avoid criminal liability for transacting with sanctioned individuals or entities
- Detect PEPs that require enhanced due diligence
- Identify clients listed in the SAT 69-B for non-existent operations

## Databases Covered

| Database          | Source                                         | Coverage                                                          |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **OFAC SDN**      | U.S. Treasury Office of Foreign Assets Control | Individuals, entities, vessels, aircraft globally                 |
| **UNSC**          | UN Security Council                            | Sanctions regimes for multiple countries and groups               |
| **SAT 69-B**      | Mexican Tax Authority (SAT)                    | Mexican taxpayers with non-existent or simulated operations       |
| **PEP**           | AI-powered database                            | Politically Exposed Persons — current and former public officials |
| **Adverse Media** | AI-powered web analysis                        | News articles linking individuals/entities to criminal activity   |
