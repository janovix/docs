# Settings Manual

The **Settings** application is your control center for managing your Janovix account, organization, team members, compliance configuration, and billing.

Access it at **[auth.janovix.com/settings](https://auth.janovix.com/settings)**, or from any Janovix application by clicking your avatar in the top-right corner and selecting **Settings**.

## Navigation

The Settings sidebar has two sections:

### User Settings

Applies to your personal account across all organizations:

- **Personal** — Profile, avatar, timezone, theme, language
- **Billing** — Your subscription plan, usage metrics, and payment

### Organization Settings

Applies to the currently selected organization:

- **Organization** — Name, logo, timezone, clock format
- **AML Compliance** — Activity code, RFC, KYC self-service configuration
- **Team** — Members, roles, and pending invitations
- **API Keys** — Create and manage API keys for programmatic access
