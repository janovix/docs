# Welcome to Janovix Docs

Janovix is a compliance platform designed to help Mexican businesses subject to the **Ley Federal para la Prevención e Identificación de Operaciones con Recursos de Procedencia Ilícita (LFPIORPI)** fulfill their anti-money laundering (AML) and Know Your Customer (KYC) obligations — efficiently and at scale.

This documentation covers everything you need to get started and master the platform.
